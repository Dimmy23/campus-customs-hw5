101 · Bulldog tee

Customer order from Tauhid Zaman for SKU CC-TEE-WHITE, size S, quantity 1.

Expected

  • Order: Boss → Inventory → Accounting → Customer Service
  • First call: Inventory, because we first want to confirm that size S is really out of stock (we expect 0). If it is, Accounting then checks the reprint to estimate when new shirts could arrive.
  • Agents:
    • Boss: opens ticket 101 to see the request and what is attached to it (invoice 501, a rush reprint). Tool: get_ticket_context.
    • Inventory: checks how many size-S tees are in stock and which other sizes are available as alternatives. Tool: check_inventory.
    • Accounting: checks invoice 501 (amount, due date, status) and looks up the printer and its usual lead time, to estimate roughly when size S could be back. Tools: get_ticket_context, get_vendor_details.
    • Customer Service: writes a message to Tauhid: size S is out of stock, we will contact him when it is back, please reply with an email or phone number, and would he like a different size or another item instead. No promised dates. No tools; it uses what Inventory and Accounting found.
  • Not needed: Facilities, because this ticket has nothing to do with the lease or rent.
  • Needs a person: the agents can't confirm whether or when the reprint will arrive (invoice 501 is unpaid and overdue), so the Boss flags this for a person to check with the printer.
  • MCP tools expected: get_ticket_context, check_inventory, get_vendor_details. Not expected: list_open_tickets.

Actual

  • Agents worked: Boss, Inventory, Accounting, and Customer Service. Facilities was not needed.
  • Delegation: Boss delegated to Accounting and Customer Service.
  • Tools used: Boss used get_ticket_context, check_inventory, and get_vendor_details. Accounting used get_ticket_context and get_vendor_details. Customer Service used get_ticket_context. Inventory used check_inventory, get_ticket_context, get_vendor_details, and list_open_tickets.
  • Outcome: MCP data confirmed size-S quantity 0; invoice 501 is open for $840.00 and linked to vendor 1, Bulldog Print Co. No reprint or arrival date was promised. Human review remains required.

102 · Rent due

Rent notice from Elm City Properties, linked to lease 1 for the Chapel Street shop.

Expected

  • Order: Boss → Facilities → Accounting
  • First call: Facilities, because we first need to confirm from the lease record that rent is really due, how much, and when. Only then does Accounting look at the money side.
  • Agents:
    • Boss: opens ticket 102 to see the notice and the lease it links to. Tool: get_ticket_context.
    • Facilities: confirms from lease 1 the space, landlord, monthly rent and next due date (we expect $2,400 due September 2). Tool: get_ticket_context.
    • Accounting: checks whether the shop has enough cash for the rent and whether a rent payment is already recorded. None of the current MCP tools reads cash or payment records, so we expect Accounting to report that it can't verify this. Accounting does not pay or schedule the rent. Tool: get_ticket_context (for the rent amount).
  • Not needed: Inventory, because no products are involved. Customer Service, because there is no customer to reply to.
  • Needs a person: paying the rent. The Boss flags the payment for a person to approve and make before September 2.
  • MCP tools expected: get_ticket_context. Not expected: list_open_tickets, check_inventory, get_vendor_details.

Actual

  • Agents worked: Boss, Facilities, Accounting, Inventory, and Customer Service.
  • Delegation: Boss delegated to Accounting and Facilities. Facilities delegated to Accounting. Inventory and Customer Service also delegated to Accounting during the run.
  • Tools used: Boss did not use an MCP tool directly. Facilities, Accounting, Inventory, and Customer Service each used get_ticket_context. No other MCP tool was used.
  • Outcome: Lease 1 confirmed the Chapel Street shop, Elm City Properties, $2,400 monthly rent, and a 2026-09-02 due date. Payment status was not verifiable from the available context. No payment was made; human approval remains required.

103 · Bulk hoodie discount

Price-override request from Yale AI Club for SKU CC-HOOD-NAVY, size M, quantity 20.

Expected

  • Order: Boss → Inventory → Accounting → Customer Service
  • First call: Inventory, because we first need to confirm how many size-M hoodies we have (we expect 8) and how many we are short (we expect 12).
  • Agents:
    • Boss: opens ticket 103 to see the request. Tool: get_ticket_context.
    • Inventory: confirms the size-M stock and the shortfall against the 20 requested. It does not order more. Tool: check_inventory.
    • Accounting: uses the hoodie's unit cost and list price to calculate revenue and margin at full price and at a few possible discount levels. These are calculations only, not an approved discount. Tool: get_ticket_context (for pricing).
    • Customer Service: writes the Yale AI Club a reply that acknowledges the request and says we are reviewing stock and pricing. No promised discount, reservation or delivery date. No tools.
  • Not needed: Facilities, because this ticket has nothing to do with the lease or rent.
  • Needs a person: approving any discount, ordering the 12 missing hoodies, and giving an arrival date. No supplier is linked to the hoodie in the database, so the agents can't estimate when more would arrive.
  • MCP tools expected: get_ticket_context, check_inventory. Not expected: list_open_tickets, get_vendor_details.

Actual

  • Agents worked: Boss, Inventory, Accounting, and Facilities. Customer Service was not present in the final recorded findings.
  • Delegation: Boss delegated to Accounting and Inventory. Inventory delegated to Boss.
  • Tools used: Boss used get_ticket_context. Inventory used get_ticket_context and check_inventory. Accounting used get_ticket_context and check_inventory. Facilities used get_ticket_context.
  • Outcome: MCP data confirmed 8 size-M hoodies in Aisle A, leaving a shortfall of 12 against the requested 20. Pricing was $22 unit cost and $58 list price; discount scenarios were analysis only. No purchase or discount was approved.
